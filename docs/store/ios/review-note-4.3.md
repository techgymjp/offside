## 返信(英語)

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for your review. We would like to explain why Offside Trap is a unique app and not a copy or re-skin of another app.

1. A concept no other app of ours has
Offside Trap is a defending game built entirely around the offside rule (Law 11 of the Laws of the Game). The player controls the back line: at the exact moment a pass is played, they tap the defenders who should step up, to leave the attackers in an offside position. The core skill is timing and reading the line, not shooting or scoring.

2. Gameplay and rules engine made for this app
- The offside decision is computed at the moment of the kick from the real conditions of Law 11: the second-last opponent, the ball, the halfway line, and "level is onside".
- 13 different situations appear in random order, with randomized player positions every time: through balls, long balls, cut-backs, a keeper off his line, attackers starting in their own half, and more.
- Situations where offside cannot be called by the rules (throw-ins, goal kicks, corner kicks) must be defended differently, with a separate MARK action. The game teaches when the trap works and when it does not.
- Stepping up too many defenders is penalized: an attacker exploits the space behind them.
- After every play, a VAR-style replay shows the offside line and the gap in centimeters, and the assistant referee raises the flag. Each result comes with a short explanation of the rule that applied.

3. Relationship to our other app
Our app "Corner Kick" is also a football game, but its gameplay is the opposite: the player attacks, delivering corner kicks and scoring with headers and slides. Offside Trap has no shooting at all. The two apps share only our in-house 3D stadium graphics; the game design, controls, rules logic, scoring and content are different.

4. Not a template or white-label app
Offside Trap was designed and developed by our own team. It was not generated from a template, and it is not a re-branded version of another app.

The current build has the same features during and after review. If any specific part of the app looks similar to something else, please let us know and we will gladly explain or change it.

Best regards,

2. このアプリのために作ったゲームとルールの判定
- オフサイドの判定は、キックの瞬間に第11条の条件(後ろから2人目の相手、ボール、ハーフウェーライン、「並んでいればオンサイド」)で計算します。
- 13 種類の場面がランダムな順番で、毎回ちがう選手の配置で出ます(スルーパス、ロングボール、折り返し、キーパーが前に出た場面、自陣から走り出す攻撃など)。

- ディフェンダーを余計に上げると、空いたスペースを突かれて失点します。
- プレーのたびに、VAR のようにオフサイドラインと差(cm)を表示し、副審が旗を上げます。結果ごとに、どのルールが当てはまったかの短い解説が出ます。
